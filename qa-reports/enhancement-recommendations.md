# Enhancement recommendations

Prioritize the recorded access, data-integrity, replay and analysis failures. Preserve working page layouts and original SVG assets. Do not add more similar games to increase catalog size.

After functional regression, redesign a small pilot: Photo Mission with an editable frame, Fashion Studio with a rendered outfit, Science Lab with controlled variables and observations, Mini Entrepreneur with inventory/pricing/customer consequences, and Eco Rescue with sorting and resource trade-offs. Keep Code the Robot's executable command mechanic, but expand solvable layouts and clear error feedback.

Use 18 focused headline games across eight domains as proposed in the [Stage 1 audit](../docs/audit/STAGE1_RECOVERY_AUDIT.md). Merges/hiding remain recommendations requiring approval; none are applied here. Maintain free selection of all four levels.

Persist voluntary selections, source of recommendation, dates, challenge versions, enjoyment and task-specific outcomes. Normalize exposure, keep unscored creativity unscored, and explain recommendations through actual records. A three-click or three-session threshold must not become a validated career assessment. Do not claim the disconnected synthetic RandomForest improves interest discovery.

Before public deployment, separately review guardian verification, rate limiting, secret management, origin policy, H2 console exposure, retention/deletion, backups, privacy documentation and recovery. The local QA run does not certify production readiness.
